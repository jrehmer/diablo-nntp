- dsyncgroups: a line of `LIST NEWSGROUPS`, `LIST ACTIVE` or a `-G` file longer than 4095 bytes (before its line end) was cut in two, and the second part was read as a line of its own: with `-g` its first word became a newsgroup (a junk group such as `%=` in `dactive.kp`). Such a line is now skipped whole, with a note. Also, `LIST NEWSGROUPS` only sets the descriptions of groups that exist or that `LIST ACTIVE` returned; a group named only there is no longer created. With `-D`, dsyncgroups deletes no group when a list line was skipped as too long, since the list is then incomplete (D-320).
- dgrpctl: `checkgroups` (without `exec`) and `masscancel` printed their output while they held the group's record lock, so a stdout that does not drain (a pager left unscrolled) kept every feeder and reader that writes to that group waiting. They print after releasing the lock now; the output is the same (D-321).
