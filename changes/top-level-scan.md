At the top only the over and data files of groups whose directory is the top, and the temporary files `dexpireover` leaves for them, are acted on: an admin's files there stay untouched (other names, the file names of groups that live in a subdirectory, an over file whose header does not name the group of its name), and after a switch between `hierarchy` and `hierarchy-full` the old files at the top are left alone as in subdirectories. With `dexpireover -p N` one worker does the top. crc and md5 spools are not affected (D-338).
