- `diablo`: new `textbinary <size>` option of the `IFILTER` label in `dnewsfeeds`. An article with text in its body before a small binary (a uuencoded, BinHex or base64 file posted with its discussion, as in early groups such as comp.sources.* and comp.sys.mac.*) is no longer rejected as `IncomingFilter` when its encoded data (the lines of its runs of 9 or more uuencode, BinHex or base64 lines and the short lines that end them, and every encoded line after the binary starts, without line ends) is at most `<size>` bytes; the label is then checked again with the article's type without its binary types, so it still filters for its other types. Text after the binary does not count. An article that is only a binary, one with no text before its binary, a yEnc binary, an `application/octet-stream` part and text that decodes to binary data are rejected as before. With the option, `lib/arttype.c` reads a binary article with text before its binary on to its end to measure it (until the limit is passed); one with no text before its binary is not read on; the type it reports stays the one the article had when it became a binary. Off by default. `dfeedtest -a` shows the type the label is checked again with. A limit of 2g or more is no longer taken for off, and `textbinary` in a label other than `IFILTER` is ignored with a warning. Tests: `TestIncomingFilterTextBinary`, `TestTextBinaryArtType`.
