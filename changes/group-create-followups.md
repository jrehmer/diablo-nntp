- Follow-ups of the create-if-absent group creation (D-312, D-315, D-316): `checkgroups` (the control message and `dgrpctl checkgroups exec`) now checks a group that was made by another creator in the meantime (an auto-add as a plain `y` group, say) as it does any existing group, so a `(Moderated)` group gets status `m` and the list's description at once instead of at the next checkgroups; its article numbers are still left alone, and the log says "was made meanwhile, checked as an existing group". `dsyncgroups -g` makes the record of a new group in one append, with all its fields, so a `dsyncgroups` that is killed leaves no group with NB but no NE or NX (`lib/kpdb.c` has a new `KPDBCreateRecord()` for it), and with `-v` it prints the group's line only after the record is made, so a stdout that does not drain no longer holds the group locked for the feeder and dreaderd. Tests: `TestGroupCreateCheckgroupsMeanwhileDgrpctl`, `TestGroupCreateCheckgroupsMeanwhileControl`, `TestGroupCreateDsyncgroupsLock`, `TestGroupCreateDsyncgroupsKilled`.
