- `parsedate()` (the age check of incoming articles, `didate`, the reader's article-age log) also reads the RFC 850 form with hyphens (`Monday, 01-Jan-01 12:00:00 GMT`) and the ctime(3) and date(1) forms (`Mon Jan  1 12:00:00 2001`, `Mon Jan  1 12:00:00 UTC 2001`, the zone name is optional; an unknown zone name there makes the date unreadable). Old Usenet articles carry these. Before, they were unreadable: accepted unchecked with `strictdate no` (an old or future article of this form passed the age limit) and rejected with `strictdate yes`. The same calendar, zone and two-digit-year rules apply as for the RFC 5322 form; ISO 8601, US and Unix-time dates are still not read.
