- Group creation is create-if-absent (D-254): a process that made a group because it found no record for it no longer writes NB and NE over a group another process made, and numbered, in between. Before, a newgroup control message (or a checkgroups, or `dgrpctl newgroup`, `dgrpctl checkgroups exec`, `dsyncgroups -g`, `readerautoaddtoactive`, `feederautoaddtoactive`) racing with another creator could set NB=1 and NE=0 on a group with articles, so article numbers were given twice. A new `KPDBCreate()` makes the record only if there is none, looking again under the append lock, and every creator uses it; on a group that exists by then, newgroup (control message and `dgrpctl`) goes on as it does for any existing group (moderator, description, status, times), and the others leave it as it is (`dsyncgroups` prints "made meanwhile, left as it is", `checkgroups` "was made meanwhile, left as it is"). The numbers `dsyncgroups -N...` sets on a group that existed at its scan are unchanged. Tests: `TestGroupCreateDgrpctlNewgroup`, `TestGroupCreateDgrpctlCheckgroups`, `TestGroupCreateDsyncgroups`, `TestGroupCreateControlNewgroup`, `TestGroupCreateControlCheckgroups`, `TestGroupCreateReaderAutoAdd`, `TestGroupCreateFeederAutoAdd`.
