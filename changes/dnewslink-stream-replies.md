- dnewslink closes the connection at once when a stream reply matches no pending article in the state it answers (239 or 439 to a CHECK, 238 or 438 to a TAKETHIS, or a Message-ID that is not pending), and offers the pending articles again on the next connection; before it waited for the read timeout (`-t`, 600 s by default) for the reply that never came.
- dnewslink keeps an article in the queue file when its TAKETHIS gets 431 and the article was deferred already (or the answer comes while the deferred articles are sent again): it is sent on a later run. Before it was rejected, so the article was lost. A realtime feed has no queue to write to and still rejects it.
- A deferred article that is pending when the connection is lost keeps its "deferred once" mark across the reconnect, so the peer cannot make dnewslink defer it again.
